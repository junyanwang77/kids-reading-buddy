Niuniu's Minecraft House

2026-08-02 的阅读练习

1. 短文 / 朗读版本

Yesterday, I wanted to build a house in Minecraft for my dog Niuniu. I did not have any ideas, so I borrowed an idea from my friend Tom's world. First, I built a small red house with a soft bed inside. After that, I added a blue door and a garden with yellow flowers. My dog Niuniu sat next to me and watched the screen. I wanted to add a swimming pool too, but my mom said it was time for dinner. I felt a little disappointed because I was not completely finished. Instead, I saved my game and told Niuniu about my plan. "Tonight we will finish the pool," I said. Niuniu barked happily, like she understood every word.
