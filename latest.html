Niuniu Waits by the Pool

2026-08-04 的阅读练习

1. 短文 / 朗读版本

Last Sunday, I went to the pool for my swimming lesson. I forgot my goggles at home, so I asked my friend Amy to borrow hers. She said yes and looked happy for me. My coach taught us a new way to kick our legs. First, I tried it, but I was too slow. I closed my eyes and imagined myself swimming like a fish, very fast. After that, I tried again, and I swam all the way across the whole pool without stopping! I felt very happy. When the lesson finished, I saw Niuniu waiting by the door outside the pool. She jumped up and ran around me, happy. I told her about my swim, and she put her head on my leg and listened. Next week, I want to borrow Amy's goggles again and try to swim even faster.

3. 阅读理解题

5. 三句话写作

Write three sentences about a time you tried something new or difficult, like swimming a new way or practicing a hard piano piece.

  • Sentence 1: Say what happened. (One day, I tried ___ and it was hard. I ___.)
  • Sentence 2: Say what happened next, using a connector like 'after that' or 'then'. (After that, I ___.)
  • Sentence 3: Say how you felt, using 'because'. (I felt ___ because ___.)
