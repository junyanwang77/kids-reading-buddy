The Castle That Disappeared

2026-07-31 的阅读练习

1. 短文 / 朗读版本

Yesterday, I played Minecraft on my dad's tablet. I wanted to build a big castle with blue walls. First, I imagined how tall and beautiful it would look. I put the blocks together one by one, and Niuniu sat next to me and watched the screen. After I worked for a long time, a creeper came near my castle. Boom! My castle was completely gone. I felt very disappointed because I worked so hard on it. Instead of giving up, I asked my friend Tom for help. He let me borrow his idea to build a stone wall around the castle. We built it again together, and this time it was safe. Niuniu wagged her tail like she was happy too!

2. 生词解释

  • imagine /ˌɪˈmæʤən/ 想象
    First, I imagined how tall and beautiful it would look.
  • completely /kəmˈplitli/ 完全地
    My castle was completely gone.
  • disappointed /ˌdɪsəˈpɔɪnɪd/ 失望的
    I felt very disappointed because I worked so hard on it.
  • instead /ˌɪnˈstɛd/ 而不是，代替
    Instead of giving up, I asked my friend Tom for help.
  • borrow /ˈbɑˌroʊ/ 借（东西/主意）
    He let me borrow his idea to build a stone wall around the castle.
  • wagged /wægd/ （尾巴）摇摆
    Niuniu wagged her tail like she was happy too!

3. 阅读理解题

  • What did the child want to build in Minecraft, and who sat next to him and watched?
  • What happened to the castle after the creeper came near it?
  • Why do you think the child asked Tom for help instead of just building the castle alone again?
  • How did the child's feelings change from the middle of the story to the end?
