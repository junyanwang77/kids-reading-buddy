The Piano Recital Surprise

2026-07-29 的阅读练习

1. 短文 / 朗读版本

Yesterday, I practiced a new piano song for my school recital. I imagined myself playing it perfectly on the stage. First, I played the beginning part well, and my dog Niuniu lay under the piano and listened quietly. After that, I tried the fast part, but my fingers got confused and I made three mistakes in a row. I felt very disappointed because I wanted to play well for my mom. Instead of giving up, I asked my piano teacher for help. She let me borrow an easier version of the song to practice first. I played it again and again after dinner. Slowly, my fingers remembered where to go. Two days later, I played the whole song without a single mistake, and Niuniu wagged her tail happily!

2. 生词解释

  • imagine /ˌɪˈmæʤən/ 想象
    I imagined myself playing it perfectly on the stage.
  • disappointed /ˌdɪsəˈpɔɪnɪd/ 失望的
    I felt very disappointed because I wanted to play well for my mom.
  • borrow /ˈbɑˌroʊ/ 借
    She let me borrow an easier version of the song to practice first.
  • instead /ˌɪnˈstɛd/ 而不是
    Instead of giving up, I asked my piano teacher for help.
  • confused /kənfˈjuzd/ 困惑的，搞混的
    My fingers got confused and I made three mistakes in a row.
  • wagged /wægd/ （尾巴）摇摆
    Niuniu wagged her tail happily!

3. 阅读理解题

  • What did Niuniu do while the child played the beginning part of the song?
  • What happened when the child tried to play the fast part of the song?
  • Why did the child feel disappointed?
  • Why do you think Niuniu wagged her tail at the end of the story?

4. 口语问答（说出来，不用写）

  • Have you ever felt disappointed like this when practicing piano or something else? What did you do?
  • If you could borrow something from a friend or teacher to help you learn, what would it be?
  • Does Niuniu like listening to you play piano? What does she usually do while you practice?

5. 三句话写作

Write 3 sentences about a time you practiced something and it got easier. Use past tense.

  • First, I ___ (what did you try to do?)
  • After that, ___ (what happened next? use 'and' or 'after that')
  • I felt ___ because ___ (how did you feel, and why?)
