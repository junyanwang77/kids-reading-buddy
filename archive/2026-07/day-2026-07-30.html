Niuniu's First Swim

2026-07-30 的阅读练习

1. 短文 / 朗读版本

Last Saturday, I went to the lake with my dog, Niuniu. Niuniu is a big golden retriever with soft yellow fur. At first, Niuniu was nervous. She stood by the water and did not move. I said, "Come on, Niuniu! It's fun!" But she just looked at me. I forgot my goggles, so I asked my friend Amy to borrow hers. She said yes. After that, I jumped into the lake. The water was cool and nice. Then something great happened. Niuniu ran into the water too! She swam next to me, and her tail went fast in the water. I was disappointed at first because Niuniu did not want to swim. But in the end, it was the best day ever!
