Niuniu Goes to the Pool

2026-07-28 的阅读练习

1. 短文 / 朗读版本

This morning, my dog Niuniu came with me to the swimming pool for the first time. She looked nervous when she saw the big blue water. I picked her up and put her in slowly. At first she was scared, but then she started to swim! Her legs moved fast, and her tail went completely wet. I laughed and shouted, "Good girl, Niuniu!" After that, we played with a ball in the water. She was so happy that she didn't want to get out. Instead of going home right away, we sat by the pool and ate ice cream together. When we finally left, Niuniu looked back at the pool. I think she wants to come back again next week!

2. 生词解释

  • nervous /ˈnərvəs/ 紧张的
    She looked nervous when she saw the big blue water.
  • scared /skɛrd/ 害怕的
    At first she was scared, but then she started to swim!
  • completely /kəmˈplitli/ 完全地
    Her tail went completely wet.
  • instead /ˌɪnˈstɛd/ 代替，而不是
    Instead of going home right away, we sat by the pool.
  • pool /pul/ 游泳池
    My dog Niuniu came with me to the swimming pool.
  • tail /teɪl/ 尾巴
    Her legs moved fast, and her tail went completely wet.

3. 阅读理解题

  • Why did Niuniu look nervous when she first saw the pool?
  • What happened right after Niuniu started to swim?
  • What did the family do instead of going straight home?
  • Why do you think Niuniu looked back at the pool when they finally left?

4. 口语问答（说出来，不用写）

  • Have you ever felt nervous about trying something new, like Niuniu at the pool? What happened?
  • If you could teach Niuniu one new trick, what would it be and why?
  • Do you like swimming with Niuniu or your family? Tell me about a fun day at the pool.

5. 三句话写作

Write 3 sentences about a time you or Niuniu tried something new or a little scary. Use past tense.

  • First, ___ (what happened at the beginning?)
  • After that, ___ (what happened next? use 'and' or 'after that')
  • I felt ___ because ___ (how did you feel, and why?)
