Niuniu and the Minecraft Castle

2026-07-28 的阅读练习

1. 短文 / 朗读版本

Last Saturday, Tom wanted to finish his Minecraft castle before dinner. He needed just ten more blocks, but his dog Niuniu kept barking at the door. Tom felt a little nervous because Niuniu never barked like that. He looked outside and saw dark clouds. Instead of finishing his castle, Tom put down his tablet and took Niuniu for a walk before the rain started. On the way, Niuniu jumped into a puddle and got completely wet. Tom laughed and said, 'Now we both need a bath!' When they got home, Tom's castle was still there, waiting. He felt happy that he chose Niuniu instead of Minecraft. Tomorrow, he can imagine a bigger castle - with a doghouse right next to it.

2. 生词解释

  • nervous 紧张的
    Tom felt a little nervous because Niuniu never barked like that.
  • instead 代替；而不是
    Instead of finishing his castle, Tom put down his tablet.
  • completely 完全地
    Niuniu jumped into a puddle and got completely wet.
  • imagine 想象
    Tomorrow, he can imagine a bigger castle.
  • puddle 水坑
    Niuniu jumped into a puddle on the way.
  • barking （狗）叫
    His dog Niuniu kept barking at the door.

3. 阅读理解题

  • What was Tom building before dinner?
  • What happened to Niuniu on the way to the walk?
  • Why do you think Niuniu kept barking at the door before Tom went outside?
  • How did Tom feel about choosing Niuniu instead of Minecraft, and why?

4. 口语问答（说出来，不用写）

  • Do you have a pet, or is there an animal you love like Niuniu? Tell me about it.
  • Have you ever had to stop playing a game to do something else? What happened?
  • What would you build first if you could build anything in Minecraft?

5. 三句话写作

Think about something that happened with Niuniu or your Minecraft game this week. Write 3 sentences in the past tense: what happened, what happened next, and how you felt.

  • First, ___ (what happened - use past tense, like 'played' or 'went').
  • After that, ___ (what happened next - use a connector like 'and' or 'but').
  • I felt ___ because ___ (how you felt and why).
