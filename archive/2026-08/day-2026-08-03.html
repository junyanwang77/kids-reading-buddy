The Wrong Note

2026-08-03 的阅读练习

1. 短文 / 朗读版本

Yesterday, I practiced piano for the school concert. I played the start of my song well. Then I played a wrong note, and I stopped. I felt disappointed, and I did not want to play again. My dog Niuniu came and sat next to me. She looked at me with her big eyes. I was nervous about the concert soon. Instead of stopping, I played the song again from the start. I played a wrong note two times again, but I did not stop. I tried again and again. After many tries, I played the whole song completely, with no wrong notes! Niuniu ran to me and looked happy. She put her head on my leg. I smiled and said, "Thank you, Niuniu." Now I feel ready for the concert.

3. 阅读理解题

  • What was the girl practicing, and why?
  • What happened the first time she played a wrong note?
  • What did Niuniu do while the girl was practicing?
  • The girl still felt nervous even after she played the whole song with no mistakes. Why do you think she still felt nervous about the concert?

5. 三句话写作

Write three sentences about a time you practiced something and made a mistake, like the girl in the story.

  • Sentence 1: Say what happened. (One day, I practiced ___ and I made a mistake. I ___.)
  • Sentence 2: Say what happened next, using a connector like 'after that' or 'instead'. (After that, I ___.)
  • Sentence 3: Say how you felt, using 'because'. (I felt ___ because ___.)
