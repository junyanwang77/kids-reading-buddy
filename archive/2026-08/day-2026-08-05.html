Niuniu Wants to Play Too

2026-08-05 的阅读练习

1. 短文 / 朗读版本

Yesterday, I sat on my bed and played Minecraft on my computer. I wanted to build a big castle. My dog Niuniu came into my room. She was quiet at first, but then she barked twice. I did not stop, so she pushed my leg with her nose and made more noise. I felt bad, so I closed the game. Instead, I took Niuniu to the garden and threw her ball. She ran fast and brought it back to me. We played for thirty minutes. After that, I went back to my room and finished my castle. I was happy because I played with Niuniu and finished my game, too.
